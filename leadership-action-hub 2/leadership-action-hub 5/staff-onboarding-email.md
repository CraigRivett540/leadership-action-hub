Hi team,

The **Leadership Action Hub** is ready for Helping Heroes and Community Assist. Use it for **team tasks, requests, notes, and follow-ups**.

**Hub link:** https://leadership-action-hub.vercel.app  

Please complete your **first sign-in by [DAY]**.

### 1. Sign in (first time)

1. Open: **https://leadership-action-hub.vercel.app**  
2. Enter **your work email** (list below).  
3. Choose a **password** (min. 8 characters) — this creates your account.  
4. Click **Continue**.

Next visits: same email + password.

### 2. What you’ll see

| Area | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Overview** | Open work + live actions table |
| **All actions** | Team tasks/requests (shared) |
| **My to do · Work** | Your private work list (only you) |
| **Team names** | Open someone’s open items |
| **Groups** | Groups you belong to |
| **For review** | Items from leadership |

### 3. Everyday use

- **Assigned to you** → open → notes if needed → mark complete  
- **Send work to anyone** → **New task** or **New request** → pick any team member or group  
- **Private reminder** → **My to do · Work**  
- **Completed items** leave the live lists → use the **Closed** filter to see them again
